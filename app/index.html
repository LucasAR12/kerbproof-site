Talk to drivers in the Kerbproof app. If a driver is offline, the message goes by text. Automatic messages cover the routine: lost signal, expiring documents, pay ready.

Your company email, inside Kerbproof. Each email is sorted by AI and linked to the right place: statements go to reconciliation, fines to the driver who had the van, documents to the driver's profile.
