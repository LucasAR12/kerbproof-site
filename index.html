Questions owners ask

Short answers. If yours isn't here, ask it in the form.

Do I need to connect Kerbproof to my carrier's systems?

No. You export the statement and your delivery report as you normally would and send the files. Nothing logs into your carrier's portal.

Which files do you need?

The payment or settlement statement for the period, and your own record of what was delivered: a route report, a delivery export or the spreadsheet you already keep. CSV or Excel is fine.

Will my carrier know I used it?

No. We never contact your carrier. If there's a gap, the dispute pack is yours to send, through your normal channel.

What if you find nothing?

Then you know you were paid correctly for that month, and it cost you nothing to find out.

What does it cost after the free check?

We agree it with you after you've seen your result. The first month is free either way.

Is this only for Amazon DSPs?

No. It works with any network that sends you a statement you can export. Amazon, DPD and Evri are where we start.
